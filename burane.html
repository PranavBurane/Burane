Brand Philosophy

More Than A Culture.

Growing up as a kid, long summers were spent at my maternal grandparents' house. They had fruit trees bearing harvests sweeter than anything I’ve ever tasted—fruits ripened naturally with time, effort, and love, completely unlike the artificial ethylene-ripened chemicals found in commercial markets.

My uncles taught me the true meaning of purity—a foundational moral shaping how I think about food and fermentation. Surrounded by high-altitude Himalayan flavors like Buransh (rhododendron), kafal, malta, and hisalu raspberries, commercial juice never stood a chance.

At just 15 years old (born in 2010), the journey started in a basic home lab—armed with bare hands, raw curiosity, and a successful first batch of traditional Tepache. Over countless 500 ml micro-batches and wild ginger bug experimentation, BURANÉ was born.

The Dual Meaning of Culture

1. Microbial Culture

The living symbiotic colonies, SCOBYs, and wild ginger bugs that transform pure fruit and botanicals into living beverages.

2. Human Culture

The ancestral memories of high-altitude wild fruits, local harvesting knowledge, and highland culinary wisdom passed down generations.

Educational Archive

The Culture

Understanding our 10-day artisanal fermentation timeline and the microbial ecosystems behind every bottle.

Phase 1 (Days 1–6)

Primary Fermentation

Whole fruits and Himalayan botanicals are inoculated with wild ginger bugs or localized symbiotic cultures. During these first six days, active yeast metabolizes natural sugars, building complex organic acids, delicate aromatic esters, and foundational structure.

Phase 2 (Days 7–10)

Secondary Conditioning

Bottled under precise pressure control, the beverage undergoes natural carbonation. This secondary conditioning allows micro-bubbles to integrate smoothly into the liquid, creating a champagne-like effervescence that highlights the raw fruit notes.

Living Micro-Ecosystems

"We don't force fermentation; we guide it."

Every batch is unpasteurized and alive. By respecting ambient temperature fluctuations and utilizing raw, unrefined cane sugars and wild foraged yeasts, BURANÉ preserves the living enzymes that commercial beverages lose entirely.
