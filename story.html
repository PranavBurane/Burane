Ancestral Roots

Granny's Trees &
The Soil That Raised Us

Long before the bottles, the fermentations, and the laboratory in Rishikesh, there were the ancient fruit trees planted by grandmother in the high-altitude Himalayan air.

The Origin

Where Every Ferment Begins

The inspiration for BURANÉ isn't found in modern commercial recipes—it is rooted right in the earth of our family's ancestral land. Granny’s trees have weathered decades of crisp Himalayan winters, torrential monsoon rains, and intense mountain sun.

These old-growth trees yield fruit with an unmatched mineral depth and concentrated wild character. When harvested at peak ripeness, they carry the true taste of the mountains that artificial flavorings can never replicate.

The Old Orchard, Uttarakhand
Wild Foraging & Care
Generational Stewardship

Preserving the Wild Heritage

Pranav founded BURANÉ to honor this exact lineage. By taking the wild, complex fruits from these protected family trees and introducing them to natural wild fermentation, we transform seasonal abundance into a living beverage.

Every batch respects the rhythm of nature—we only harvest what the trees provide, ensuring the orchards remain healthy and balanced for generations to come.
